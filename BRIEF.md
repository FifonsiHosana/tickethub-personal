## Project Brief: TICKETHUB GHANA

## Project Overview

Build a web-based event management and ticketing platform that enables organizers to create, manage, promote, and sell tickets for events while providing attendees with a seamless ticket-purchasing experience.

The platform should include a comprehensive organizer dashboard for tracking sales, attendees, revenue, event performance, and operational metrics in real time.

## Objectives

## For Organizers

- Create and manage events.

- Sell tickets online.

- Monitor ticket sales and revenue.

- Track attendee registrations.

- Scan and validate tickets at event entry.

- Access reports and analytics.

- Manage event staff and permissions.

## For Attendees

- Discover events.

- Purchase tickets securely.

- Receive digital tickets.

- Access ticket history.

- Transfer or share tickets where permitted.

## For Platform Administrators

- Manage users and organizers.

- Monitor platform activity.

- Handle disputes and refunds.

- Configure fees and commissions.

- Access platform-wide analytics.
